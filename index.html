TRUST BY DESIGN

Diagnosis is a process, not a guess.

01

Prefer primary sources

Manufacturer manuals, official troubleshooting articles and parts diagrams come first.

02

Trace technical claims

Record source links, model applicability, revision details and verification dates when available.

03

Safety before testing

Hazardous procedures require appropriate training and model-specific service instructions.

04

No guessed part numbers

A symptom is not proof of a failed component. Confirm with suitable tests and documentation.
